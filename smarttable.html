How it works

Three steps, no new hardware for the customer.

01 / SCAN

Scan the table's code

Every table carries its own QR code. Customers scan it with any phone camera — no app to install, no account to create.

02 / REVIEW

See the itemised bill

The order, quantities, tax and service charge appear instantly — the same bill a server would print, just on the customer's own screen.

03 / PAY

Pay with M-Pesa or card

One tap sends an STK push, or the customer pays by card. Staff see the table settle in real time — no reconciliation needed.
